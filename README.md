## 🔬 EMPIRICAL RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

### 1. Deep Reinforcement Learning on High-Frequency Limit Order Books
**Repository:** [`DeepRL_Microstructure`](./DeepRL_Microstructure)
*   **Abstract:** An empirical framework for applying Deep Reinforcement Learning (Proximal Policy Optimization) to extract generalized alpha from Level 2 Order Books.
*   **Findings:** The neural architecture successfully identified a structural imbalance edge (+10.34% OOS). However, thermodynamic sensitivity analysis proved this edge decays linearly against centralized exchange taker fees, requiring sub-3 bps institutional execution tiers or DeFi mempool latency advantages to remain profitable.

### 2. Polymarket Asynchronous Execution Engine (Live Infrastructure)
**Repository:** [`Polymarket-Arbitrage-Bot`](./Polymarket-Arbitrage-Bot)
*   **Abstract:** An autonomous, event-driven statistical arbitrage engine deployed on continuous prediction markets.
*   **Methodology:** Combines WebSocket tick ingestion with asynchronous transaction execution on the Polygon network. The engine mathematically models execution friction, proving capability in managing real-world latency desyncs and slippage erosion outside of academic Jupyter environments.

### 3. Bithax (Deep RL & High-Frequency Slippage)
**Repository:** [`bithax`](./bithax)
*   **Abstract:** A study on the exponential decay of predictive neural networks against order-book slippage.
*   **Findings:** Modeled a strict mathematical capacity ceiling (~$43.5k AUM) for a Bitcoin HFT PPO agent. Demonstrated that beyond this threshold, market-taking impact completely negates the neural network's predictive yield.

### 4. HOPE (Probabilistic Signal & Overfitting Prevention)
**Repository:** [`HOPE`](./HOPE)
*   **Abstract:** An empirical analysis of structural overfitting in single-modal deep learning models applied to noisy financial time series.
*   **Findings:** Proved the catastrophic failure of unconstrained LSTM architectures to generalize out-of-sample during macroeconomic regime shifts. Demonstrated that highly regularized, rigid constraints (Linear Regression/SVR) yield tighter confidence intervals, necessitating the fusion of macroscopic sentiment (NLP) to resolve structural blindness.

### 5. Statistical Arbitrage & Cointegration
**Repository:** [`StatArb_Crypto`](./StatArb_Crypto)
*   **Abstract:** A market-neutral pairs trading engine utilizing rigorous econometric cointegration tests (Engle-Granger/Johansen) to mathematically guarantee mean reversion.
*   **Methodology:** Dynamically calculates OLS hedge ratios and executes on rolling Z-scores, strictly factoring in execution friction constraints (0.02% exchange fees).

### 6. Tail-Risk Hedging & Gamma Scalping
**Repository:** [`Quant_Risk_Modeling`](./Quant_Risk_Modeling)
*   **Abstract:** Monte Carlo simulation pipelines designed to prove portfolio survivability against extreme market volatility.
*   **Methodology:** Implements dynamic "Insurance Protocols" (Gamma Scalping) against directional exposure, calculating the exact stochastic probability of surviving flash crashes while utilizing XGBoost regularization to eliminate curve-fitting bias.

### 7. Expected Value Modeling & Momentum
**Repository:** [`Poly-5m-Momentum-Research`](./Poly-5m-Momentum-Research)
*   **Abstract:** A research pipeline mapping the Expected Value (EV) of localized short-term momentum anomalies.
*   **Findings:** Analyzed two years of tick data to prove a non-reversion probability of 85.5% under strict execution constraints (65-cent fill parameters).
